# Code of Conduct

Kboard adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its Code of Conduct. Read the full text at that link; it applies to all
project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and community channels).

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming to everyone, whatever their background.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or publishing others'
  private information.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to **dev@thekami.tech**. Reports are handled
privately. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may temporarily
or permanently ban contributors who violate this code.
